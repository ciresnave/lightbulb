On  the  cover:  Predicting  individual  characteristics  from  brain  scans  holds  immense
potential  for  advancing  diagnostics  and  personalized  medicine.  While  large-scale
consortium  datasets  have  significantly  advanced  these  tools,  certain  clinically  relevant
measures,  such  as  inhibitory  control,  still  struggle  with  low  predictive  accuracy.  In  this
issue  of  Trends  in  Cognitive  Sciences,  Hyejin  J.  Lee  and  colleagues  highlight  that  one
major  limitation  is  the  insufficient  data  collected  per  participant.  Precision  datasets
that gather ample data to enhance the precision of individual measures can serve as a
valuable complement to consortium data. The cover illustration symbolizes this concept:
just as a telescope reveals intricate details of the brain, collecting sufficient data enhances
our insights, advancing the detection of critical findings with greater detail and precision.
