## TL;DR

Centaur is a foundation model of human cognition, fine-tuned from Llama 3.1 70B on the large-scale Psych-101 dataset. It predicts and simulates human behavior across a wide range of psychological experiments, outperforming existing cognitive models and generalizing to new domains, cover stories, and task modifications.

## Why it matters

A unified computational model that can predict human behavior in any domain is a major step toward integrated theories of cognition. Centaur demonstrates that large language models, when fine-tuned on diverse behavioral data, can capture and generalize human decision-making, learning, and reasoning, providing new tools for cognitive science and AI alignment.

## Key technical takeaways

- **Large-Scale Dataset:**
  - Psych-101 covers 160 experiments, 60,000+ participants, and 10M+ choices, transcribed into natural language.
- **Model Architecture:**
  - Centaur is built on Llama 3.1 70B, fine-tuned using QLoRA with low-rank adapters for parameter-efficient training.
- **Generalization:**
  - Predicts held-out participants, experiments, and new domains; adapts to modified cover stories and problem structures.
- **Neural Alignment:**
  - Internal representations become more aligned with human neural activity after fine-tuning.
- **Case Study:**
  - Centaur can guide the development of cognitive theories by simulating and analyzing human behavior.

## Implementation steps (Candle/Rust context)

1. **Dataset Preparation:**
   - Curate and transcribe large-scale behavioral datasets into natural language format.
2. **Model Selection:**
   - Use a state-of-the-art LLM (e.g., Llama 3.1 70B) as the backbone.
3. **Fine-Tuning:**
   - Apply QLoRA or similar parameter-efficient methods to train on behavioral data, focusing on human responses.
4. **Evaluation:**
   - Test generalization to held-out participants, experiments, and domains; analyze neural alignment.
5. **Theory Development:**
   - Use the model to simulate and analyze cognitive phenomena, supporting theory building.
