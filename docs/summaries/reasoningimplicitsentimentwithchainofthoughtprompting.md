## TL;DR

This paper introduces a Three-hop Reasoning (THOR) framework using chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting to detect implicit sentiment in text. By guiding large language models (LLMs) through aspect, opinion, and polarity inference, THOR achieves state-of-the-art results in implicit sentiment analysis (ISA).

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users working on sentiment analysis or reasoning tasks can benefit from multi-hop CoT prompting strategies. Integrating THOR-like frameworks enables more accurate detection of implicit sentiment and improves model interpretability for complex NLP tasks.

## Key technical takeaways

- Implicit sentiment analysis requires common-sense and multi-hop reasoning to infer latent intent.
- THOR framework uses three-step CoT prompting: aspect identification, opinion inference, and polarity prediction.
- Self-consistency and supervised revising mechanisms further improve reasoning accuracy.
- THOR achieves significant improvements over previous baselines, especially in zero-shot settings.
- The approach is broadly applicable to other reasoning-heavy NLP tasks.
