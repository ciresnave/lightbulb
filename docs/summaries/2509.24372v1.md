Introduces a hybrid eviction policy combining frequency and recency with light-weight predictive aging to reduce cache churn in streaming workloads.

Why it matters

- Works directly with KV cache designs (KvPager/KvCache) to lower cache miss rates under streaming generation patterns.

Actions

- Implement a simple hybrid LRU-LFU policy in `cache.rs` and compare hit/miss rates on simulated streaming traces.

Acceptance criteria

- The hybrid policy yields at least 5% reduction in miss rate on at least two streaming trace simulations vs. vanilla LRU.
