## TL;DR

This paper introduces RoG, a framework that combines large language models (LLMs) with knowledge graphs (KGs) for faithful and interpretable reasoning. RoG uses a planning-retrieval-reasoning approach to generate relation paths, retrieve valid reasoning steps, and improve LLM performance on knowledge graph question answering (KGQA).

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users can leverage RoG to enhance LLM reasoning with structured knowledge and interpretable outputs. Integrating KG-based reasoning modules can improve accuracy, trustworthiness, and explainability in high-stakes applications.

## Key technical takeaways

- LLMs are prone to hallucinations and lack up-to-date knowledge; KGs provide reliable, structured knowledge.
- RoG framework: planning (generate relation paths), retrieval (fetch valid reasoning paths), and reasoning (LLM answers based on KG structure).
- RoG distills KG knowledge into LLMs and is compatible with arbitrary LLMs at inference.
- Achieves state-of-the-art results on KGQA benchmarks, generating faithful and interpretable reasoning traces.
- Planning module is plug-and-play for different LLMs.
