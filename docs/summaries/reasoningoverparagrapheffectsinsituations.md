## TL;DR

This paper introduces ROPES, a benchmark for reading comprehension that requires reasoning about cause and effect described in expository paragraphs and applying this knowledge to novel situations. State-of-the-art models perform far below human level, highlighting the challenge of this task.

## Key technical takeaways

- ROPES focuses on reasoning over cause-effect relations in text and applying them to new situations.
- The benchmark consists of background passages, novel situations, and questions requiring causal reasoning.
- Models must go beyond local extraction to apply knowledge in context.
- SOTA models achieve only 61.6% F1, well below human performance (89.0%).
- ROPES is constructed from science textbooks and Wikipedia, with crowdsourced questions and answers.
