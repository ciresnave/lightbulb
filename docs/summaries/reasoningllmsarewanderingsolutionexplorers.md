## TL;DR

This paper argues that current reasoning LLMs (RLLMs) do not systematically explore solution spaces, but instead "wander" through reasoning steps. The authors identify common failure modes and advocate for new metrics that evaluate the structure of the reasoning process, not just final outputs.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users aiming for robust reasoning models should consider not only accuracy but also the systematic nature of solution exploration. Integrating metrics and tools to audit reasoning traces can help improve model reliability and performance on complex tasks.

## Key technical takeaways

- RLLMs often fail to systematically explore solution spaces, especially as problem complexity increases.
- Common failure modes include invalid reasoning steps, redundant explorations, hallucinated conclusions, and unfaithful outputs.
- Performance plateaus on simple tasks can mask exponential degradation on complex problems.
- Systematic exploration requires validity, effectiveness, and necessity in reasoning traces.
- The paper proposes formal definitions and auditing methods for reasoning traces.
