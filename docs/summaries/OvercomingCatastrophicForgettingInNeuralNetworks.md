## TL;DR

This paper reviews strategies to mitigate catastrophic forgetting in neural networks, a phenomenon where models lose previously learned information when trained on new tasks. It surveys regularization, rehearsal, and architectural approaches, highlighting their strengths and limitations.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users may train models on sequential tasks or in continual learning settings. Understanding and implementing anti-forgetting techniques is essential for robust model deployment, especially in dynamic or multi-task environments.

## Key technical takeaways

- Catastrophic forgetting is a major challenge in neural networks, especially for continual learning.
- Regularization-based methods (e.g., EWC) penalize changes to important weights.
- Rehearsal methods replay previous data to maintain performance.
- Architectural solutions include dynamic expansion and modularity.
- No single method is universally optimal; hybrid approaches are promising.
