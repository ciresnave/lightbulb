## TL;DR

This survey reviews neurosymbolic AI models that combine neural networks and symbolic reasoning, aiming for explainable, trustworthy, and generalizable intelligence. It classifies recent approaches, highlights key architectures, and discusses applications in learning, reasoning, and multimodal understanding.

## Why it matters (for Candle and reproducible ML)

- Candle and similar Rust ML libraries can leverage neurosymbolic methods for robust, interpretable agentic workflows and benchmarking.
- Neurosymbolic AI supports reproducible experiments by integrating learning and reasoning, improving transparency and reliability in open-source ML infrastructure.
- The taxonomy guides the design of hybrid models for explainability, fairness, and multimodal tasks.

## Key technical takeaways

- **Hybrid architectures:** Logic Tensor Networks, Neural Logic Machines, Logical Boltzmann Machines, Logic Neural Networks, and Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learners.
- **Symbolic reasoning:** Enables explainable, rule-based inference and knowledge representation.
- **Neural learning:** Provides robust pattern recognition and generalization from data.
- **Applications:** Fairness, semantic image interpretation, language understanding, entity linking, multimodal reasoning, and generative modeling.
- **Taxonomy:** Classifies models by integration strategy, reasoning capability, and application domain.
