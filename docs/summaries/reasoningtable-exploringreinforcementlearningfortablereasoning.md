## TL;DR

This paper introduces Reasoning-Table, the first application of reinforcement learning (RL) to table reasoning tasks. RL training enables large language models to outperform supervised fine-tuning (SFT) baselines, achieving state-of-the-art results and improved generalization and robustness across table QA, fact verification, and text-to-SQL tasks.
