## TL;DR

This paper presents the Self Aware Networks (SAN) theory, proposing a multiscale framework for intelligence based on oscillatory dynamics and agentic behavior across cellular, regional, and global brain scales. SAN models consciousness and cognitive control as emergent from phase wave differentials and oscillatory computational agency.

## Why it matters

Understanding the mechanisms of consciousness and intelligence requires bridging molecular, cellular, and network-level dynamics. SAN offers a unified theory that connects neural oscillations, phase synchronization, and agentic behavior, providing new insights for neuroscience, AI, and computational modeling of cognition.

## Key technical takeaways

- **Multiscale Framework:**
  - Intelligence and agency are modeled across micro (cellular), meso (regional), and macro (global brainwave) scales.
- **Oscillatory Dynamics:**
  - Phase wave differentials and traveling waves serve as information carriers, enabling functional connectivity and cognitive control.
- **Agentic Brain:**
  - Agentic behavior emerges from coordinated oscillatory patterns, with glossary and appendix detailing key terms and mechanisms.
- **Functional Connectivity:**
  - SAN describes how molecular interactions, neural arrays, and brain regions synchronize via oscillatory dynamics.
- **Consciousness Modeling:**
  - Cognitive control and consciousness are explained through top-down and bottom-up oscillatory interactions.
