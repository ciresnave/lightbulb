## TL;DR

This paper shows that explicit "thinking" steps in reasoning LLMs are not always necessary for strong performance. A simple NoThinking prompting approach can outperform traditional chain-of-thought reasoning, especially in low-latency or low-budget settings, and parallel scaling further boosts efficiency.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users can leverage NoThinking and parallel scaling strategies to achieve competitive reasoning performance with reduced latency and compute. This enables more efficient deployment of reasoning models in resource-constrained environments.

## Key technical takeaways

- NoThinking (bypassing explicit reasoning steps) can outperform traditional chain-of-thought prompting on diverse reasoning tasks.
- Parallel scaling (generating multiple outputs and aggregating) improves accuracy and latency trade-offs.
- NoThinking is especially effective in low-budget and low-latency scenarios.
- The approach provides a strong baseline for efficient reasoning without additional training or supervision.
- Results challenge the necessity of lengthy thinking processes in modern reasoning models.
