## TL;DR

This paper introduces Memory-R+, a memory-augmented reinforcement learning (RL) framework for improving chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning in tiny LLMs (<1B parameters). By leveraging episodic memory and intrinsic motivation, Memory-R+ boosts sample efficiency and generalization, making RL-based reasoning accessible in low-resource settings.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users can use Memory-R+ to fine-tune small LLMs for advanced reasoning tasks, lowering compute barriers and enabling robust model development in resource-constrained environments.

## Key technical takeaways

- RL fine-tuning is challenging for tiny LLMs due to sparse rewards and poor exploration.
- Memory-R+ uses episodic memory to store successful and failed reasoning traces, guiding exploration and exploitation.
- Intrinsic rewards are computed via kNN-based memory, encouraging alignment with successful patterns and avoidance of failures.
- Memory-R+ improves reasoning accuracy and robustness, outperforming baseline RL and handcrafted rewards.
- Enables effective RL fine-tuning for models as small as 500M parameters, democratizing advanced reasoning capabilities.
