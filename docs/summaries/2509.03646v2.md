TL;DR

2509.03646v2 evaluates cache eviction policies for KV stores in long-context models and proposes a hybrid LRU-LFU policy tuned to attention recency patterns.

Why it matters

- Direct input for `KvPager` and `KvCache` eviction heuristics.

Actions

1. Add eviction policy experiments to `bench/kvcache`.
2. Implement a configurable hybrid LRU-LFU eviction policy in `cache.rs`.

Acceptance criteria

- Benchmarks indicating improved hit-rate and reduced memory pressure compared to pure LRU on synthetic datasets.
