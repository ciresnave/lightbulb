# 2510.05949v1

TL;DR

Short systems paper profiling small-batch CPU inference and proposing per-core workload partitioning and lightweight prefetching to reduce tail latency.

Why it matters

- Tail-latency reduction techniques are crucial for deterministic CPU-first CI and production inference where single-request latency matters.
- Ideas map directly to scheduler and kernel placement decisions in `lightbulb`.

Actions

- Prototype a per-core partitioning mode toggle in the scheduler and measure tail latency on the CPU runner.
- Add a microbenchmark that simulates small-batch workloads and measures p99 latency with and without prefetching.

Acceptance criteria

- A scheduler toggle that reduces p99 latency by >= 10% on at least one small-batch microbenchmark.
