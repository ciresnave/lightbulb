## TL;DR

Reasoning Gym (RG) is a library of procedurally generated reasoning environments for reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). It provides unlimited, diverse, and automatically verifiable tasks across domains, enabling scalable training and evaluation of reasoning models.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users can leverage RG to train and benchmark reasoning models in a reproducible, scalable way. Integrating RG tasks into Candle's pipeline supports curriculum learning, dynamic evaluation, and robust model development for advanced reasoning capabilities.

## Key technical takeaways

- RG offers over 100 algorithmically verifiable tasks spanning algebra, logic, geometry, games, and more.
- Tasks are procedurally generated, eliminating memorization and enabling unlimited training data.
- Difficulty and structure are fully configurable, supporting curriculum learning and targeted evaluation.
- RLVR-trained models outperform general-purpose LLMs on reasoning tasks, but performance drops sharply with increased difficulty.
- RG enables precise experimental control and skills transfer to external benchmarks.
