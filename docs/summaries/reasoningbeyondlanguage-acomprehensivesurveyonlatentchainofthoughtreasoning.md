## TL;DR

This survey reviews the emerging field of latent Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning in large language models (LLMs), where reasoning steps occur in latent spaces rather than explicit natural language. Latent CoT enables more abstract, efficient, and flexible reasoning, but introduces new challenges in interpretability and evaluation.

## Why it matters for Candle (Rust ML library)

Candle users interested in advanced reasoning models can benefit from latent CoT techniques to improve efficiency and abstraction. Integrating latent reasoning approaches can help Candle support state-of-the-art LLM research and applications, especially where interpretability and computational cost are critical.

## Key technical takeaways

- Latent CoT decouples reasoning from language, allowing models to reason internally in latent spaces.
- Token-wise strategies include discrete tokens (e.g., pause, planning, memory) and continuous tokens (latent embeddings).
- Structural and representational innovations enable modular, efficient, and abstract reasoning.
- Challenges include lack of interpretability, evaluation gaps, and alignment risks.
- The survey provides a taxonomy, comparative analysis, and highlights open problems and future directions.
