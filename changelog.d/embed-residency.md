kind: embedding CUDA arm keeps its weights resident (InferenceContext); ROADMAP F11/F12 rows
---
The `/v1/embeddings` CUDA arm realizes through Fuel's `InferenceContext` on one held device, the only realize path that consults the per-device weight residency (fuel-tensor `const_residency`); `Tensor::realize_f32_cuda`, which it used before, never does and re-uploaded all 112 weights (546,926,592 bytes) on every request. Measured on an RTX 4070 Laptop (nomic-embed-text-v1.5, 13 tokens, `tests/nomic_gpu.rs`): request 1 uploads=112 hits=0 (2762 ms), request 2 uploads=112 hits=112, i.e. nothing uploaded (1128 ms; the old path's warm mean was 2277 ms, n=3 vs n=1, same box, other lanes also on the GPU). `NomicEmbedder::weight_residency()` reports (hits, uploads, resident bytes), and dropping the encoder clears the device's residency (fuel's unload call) so the card gets the memory back; another model on that device re-uploads its own weights next time. Control: a CPU encoder reports no residency; the test failed before the change (all counters 0).
