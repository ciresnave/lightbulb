kind: A5 logprobs on streamed chat
---
A streamed chat request with `logprobs: true` (and `top_logprobs`) now carries each token's entry on that token's own `chat.completion.chunk` (`choices[0].logprobs.content[0]`, the same entry shape as the non-streaming response); the finish chunk carries none, and a request that did not ask has no `logprobs` key on any chunk. Streaming with `response_format`, `lightbulb.output_contract`, or `tools` stays a 400 with `logprobs` (those paths replay a buffered reply). Mechanism: a new `StreamItem::Logprob` sent just ahead of its `Token` for a session that asked, fed by a new `SchedulerDriver::take_step_logprobs`. `StreamItem` is a public enum: a consumer with an exhaustive `match` needs one more arm (the bundled `prefix_sharing_bench` example has it). Verified by a handler test over the SSE frames (which caught that the streaming job was not carrying `logprobs` at all), a runner test, and a real-Fuel driver test (one entry per streamed token, none for a session that did not ask); and `a_synthetic_gguf_streams_logprobs_on_each_token_chunk` runs it through real Fuel, the real runner and the SSE handler over the synthetic GGUF (not a trained model). Completions has no streaming mode.
